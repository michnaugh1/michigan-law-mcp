# Reply from the Michigan Legislature (received 2026-09-21)

Reply to `docs/lsb-inquiry.md`, pasted by Mike Naughton. Numbers follow the questions in the letter (its questions 1-8;
the reply's answers appear to map to them one for one).

> 1. MCLs are available as machine readable xml files at the following address: https://legislature.mi.gov/documents/mcl/.
> Additionally, they are available as human readable PDFs: https://legislature.mi.gov/documents/mcl/pdf/
>
> 2. We generally ask that requests are rate-limited to a maximum of 1 request per second. This limit is not fixed and may be subject to change.
>
> 3. You may. There are no specific wording requirements.
>
> 4. There is no fixed schedule for when the MCL is updated. It depends on the activity of the Legislature and the availability of the
> compilation team. In the header of every page on the website, you can see "Michigan Compiled Laws Complete Through PA ## of ####".
> This is the best way to determine when the MCL has been updated.
>
> 5. An archival copy of the MCL is created on the first of every month. These archives date back to 2013 and are available at the
> following address: https://legislature.mi.gov/documents/mcl/archive/
>
> 6. ".amended", ".added", and ".new" sections are used to display changes that are introduced by laws that have passed but have not yet
> come into effect. For instance, Section 2.13.new was added by PA 7 of 2026, but will not go into effect until 91 days after the
> adjournment of the current session.
>
> 7. To the best of my knowledge, there is no per-change date available anywhere. Items stay in the feed for one day. The intention is that
> new documents are "added" and modified documents are "updated". "deleted" does not mean the act was repealed, it just means the document
> was removed from the website. Currently, it looks like modified items are being shown as "deleted" and then "added" instead of "updated".
> I have logged this to be fixed.
>
> 8. I do not believe we serve a robots.txt file, but automated access is permitted.

## What this changes

| Topic | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Primary source | scrape chapter HTML | **machine-readable XML** at /documents/mcl/ (structure not yet seen); chapter HTML stays as a cross-check and fallback |
| Rate | our guess | at most 1 request/second, may change; we use 2 s between requests (`MLAW_MIN_INTERVAL`) |
| Storing and serving a copy | asked | permitted, no wording requirements (we still carry the standing notice) |
| Freshness | poll the feed | the "Complete Through PA n of year" stamp is the signal; feed items last one day, so a missed day means a gap |
| History | observation dates only | monthly archives since 2013 at /documents/mcl/archive/ can backfill month-granular history |
| Variants | `-amended` only, `-added`/`[n]` guessed | `.amended`, `.added`, `.new` = passed but not yet in force |
| Feed "deleted" | maybe repeal | only "removed from the website"; a modified document currently appears as deleted then added (a known issue on their side) |
| robots.txt | 502 = assume disallow | not served, automated access permitted in writing: `MLAW_ROBOTS_ERROR_OK=1` enables proceeding when it errors; a readable robots.txt would still be honored |

Not answered: whether `[1]`-style nodes (feed shows `mcl-333-5474c[1]`) are a separate mechanism from the three suffixes above.
